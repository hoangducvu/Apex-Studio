Shipping Policy

Last updated: 4 July 2026

We ship from our hub in Malta to all EU member states and selected neighbouring countries. All prices on this site are in EUR (€) and include VAT where applicable.

Delivery times

How long a parcel is in transit depends on where it lands, not on which service carries it — both services quote the same window for a given destination.

DestinationDelivery time
Malta2–3 business days
International10–20 business days

These windows start once the parcel is posted, not from the moment you order — add the processing time below.

Delivery options & rates

We post every order with MaltaPost. You choose the service at checkout — the same two rates apply to every destination we ship to, including Malta.

ServiceWhat you getCost
StandardNo tracking€5.95
Tracked & signedTracked, signed for on delivery€13.95

Orders over €90 ship free. Standard delivery costs nothing on those orders, and Tracked & signed drops to €8.00.

Processing

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days, and you will receive a confirmation email as soon as your order ships. Tracking is only available on Tracked & signed orders; Standard is untracked, so we cannot trace it in transit.

Customs & duties

Orders shipped within the EU incur no additional customs charges. For non-EU destinations, any import duties or local taxes are the responsibility of the customer.

Questions?
